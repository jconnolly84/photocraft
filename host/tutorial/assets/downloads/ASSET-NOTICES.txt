trainer-original.png
Original product visual generated using OpenAI's built-in image_gen tool on
15 November 2026. The concept is an unbranded cobalt/lime running trainer on a
lavender-grey studio background. No real brand artwork or student images used.
The image intentionally retains its background for the removal exercise.

Generation prompt:
One photorealistic premium sportswear product photograph for a GCSE classroom
project. One original unbranded trainer, three-quarter lateral view, toe left,
whole shoe visible. Electric cobalt woven mesh, acid-lime loops/outsole,
sculpted white/charcoal sole. No real brand marks, text, feet or people. Clean
opaque lavender-grey seamless studio background, soft separated shadow, crisp
silhouette, landscape 3:2, commercial product lighting.

background-grid.png / energy-streaks.png / flux-badge.png
Original procedural graphic assets made for this project. Source script:
scripts/create_tutorial_graphics.py in the PhotoCraft repository. The streaks
use BLACK to teach Screen; the badge uses WHITE to teach Multiply. They are
not screenshots, and are not presented as PhotoCraft UI.
